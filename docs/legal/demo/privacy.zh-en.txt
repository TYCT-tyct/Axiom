1. Introduction and Scope

1.1 This Policy explains how 【operating entity】 (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data when providing the Axiom website, console, API, and related services (the “Service”), and the rights you have in that data.

1.2 This Policy applies to personal data we process when you access or use the Service. It does not apply to services independently provided by upstream model providers, third-party relay sites, payment channels, sub-site operators, or other third parties. Those third parties have their own privacy policies, which you should read separately.

1.3 “Personal data” means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as determined by 【governing law】. We process personal data in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, fairness, necessity, and good faith.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and identity data: email address, password hash, third-party login identifiers (such as GitHub, Google, or LinuxDO account identifiers), passkeys, two-step verification secrets and recovery codes, language and time-zone preferences, invitation relationships, roles and permissions, email verification status, and account and workspace identifiers.

2.2 Payment and billing data: order number, top-up amount, payment currency, payment method, payment channel, channel fee, credit credited, refunds and chargebacks, receipts, and invoice information (if you provide it). Full card numbers, CVVs, bank passwords, payment passwords, and on-chain private keys are handled by payment channels; we do not collect or store them.

2.3 API usage data: API key identifiers (prefix, last four characters, and hash), workspace, request ID, model, endpoint or source code, each attempt, usage, sale price, cost, latency, first-token time, error type, whether the call was streamed, whether it hit cache, whether it was free, client application, IP address, session and sub-agent identifiers, app-attribution headers (such as HTTP-Referer, X-OpenRouter-Title, and X-OpenRouter-Categories), and request class.

2.4 Conversation content: the complete requests you submit through the API (including messages, system prompts, tool definitions, parameters, and multimodal content such as images, PDFs, audio, and files), and the complete responses, reasoning content, and tool calls returned by models. Conversation content is collected and stored only when the platform enables storage; the platform decides as a whole, and sites and accounts cannot turn it off individually. When storage is disabled, we do not store message bodies, but we still store call records.

2.5 Support and communications data: emails, tickets, chat records, problem descriptions, attachments, and your feedback about the Service, billing, or security incidents.

2.6 Device and log data: IP address, User-Agent, access time, pages and actions, error logs, security events, login attempts, session state, CSRF tokens, and language and theme preferences. Operational logs do not contain conversation bodies, plaintext API keys, upstream keys, cookies, or authentication tokens.

2.7 Cookies and local storage: essential cookies used to maintain sessions and prevent cross-site request forgery, and browser local storage used to remember language, theme, and unfinished form state. We do not use advertising-tracking cookies or sell your personal data to advertisers.

3. How We Use Information

3.1 To provide and operate the Service: authentication, registration, routing, retries and fallbacks, billing, reservations and settlement, refunds, reconciliation, statements and receipts, and model and endpoint display.

3.2 Security and anti-abuse: preventing unauthorized access, key theft, account takeover, fraud, chargebacks, spam registration, rate-limit evasion, and other abuse; conducting security audits, incident response, and dispute investigations.

3.3 Customer support: answering your questions, troubleshooting, handling refunds and complaints, and restoring accounts.

3.4 Improving the Service: analyzing usage, performance, errors, and capacity to improve routing, endpoint quality, pricing, and user experience. We use aggregated or de-identified data where possible.

3.5 Model training and improvement: we may use stored conversation content to improve models or the Service, but only where we have the consent required by applicable law or another lawful basis; before necessary consent is obtained, we will not use conversation content for model training. Each call record indicates the training-permission status that applied at the time.

3.6 Legal and compliance: complying with laws, regulations, sanctions, export controls, law-enforcement, or regulatory requirements; enforcing these Terms; and protecting the rights, property, and safety of us, our users, and third parties.

3.7 Notices: sending service changes, security incidents, billing, refunds, account status, and important policy updates. Marketing or promotional messages require separate consent, and you can opt out at any time.

3.8 Legal bases (where applicable law requires one): performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, protection of your or another person’s vital interests, our legitimate interests (where they do not override your rights and freedoms), and your consent. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

4. Conversation Content and Call Records

4.1 Call records (metadata) are always stored and cannot be disabled. They are used for billing, settlement, reconciliation, audit, security, troubleshooting, dispute handling, and service improvement. Call records do not contain conversation bodies.

4.2 Whether conversation content is stored is decided by the platform as a whole. When storage is enabled, content is retained for 90 days by default, adjustable in the backend, and may be retained long-term; when storage is disabled, message bodies are not stored. Stored content includes complete requests, complete responses, tool calls, and multimodal content such as images and files.

4.3 Stored conversation content is isolated per account, encrypted at rest, and deduplicated by content; the deduplication fingerprint uses an HMAC-SHA256 with an account key, so someone who can list the storage bucket cannot determine whether a piece of content exists. API keys, upstream keys, cookies, authentication headers, and login tokens are not recorded.

4.4 Access restrictions: no user, customer, site administrator, or ordinary employee can view conversation bodies through the product interface or ordinary APIs. Only the platform owner, under a controlled process with two signatures from two independent keys, audit, and encrypted export, may access stored content for tracing, security, dispute handling, and, where necessary consent has been obtained, model improvement or training. Exports record the approval summary, scope, item and byte counts, output digest, and start and end audit entries.

4.5 Expiry and deletion: conversation content expires based on the last time it was referenced; after the last call referencing it expires, it is moved to cold storage or deleted by a periodic cleanup job.
